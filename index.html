          Settings

          Identification

          Each frog gets its own score, so two can pass at once. In testing, at 70% it answered 47% of recordings and was right 90% of the time. Lower answers more often but makes more mistakes.

          Location and season

          Uses iNaturalist sightings within about 50–100 km and the months each frog calls. Your location is rounded to about 1 km and stays on this phone. Turn the filter off when testing with a recording played from a speaker.

          About

          A test of identifying East Texas frogs by their calls. It runs entirely on your phone: sound never leaves the device.

          It listens in 3-second windows. Each window goes through BirdNET (an animal-sound model from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology and Chemnitz University), then a small classifier trained on about 5,000 frog recordings from iNaturalist, plus insects, night birds, rain, wind and traffic.

          BirdNET V2.4 model: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Training recordings: iNaturalist contributors (CC licences) and ESC-50 (CC BY-NC). Photos: iNaturalist contributors, credited on each frog's page. For research and education only, not for commercial use.
